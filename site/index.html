Controls

Every key is a cricket shot.

Aim with the arrows. The length of the ball decides front foot or back foot, so the same key drives a full ball and cuts a short one. Try it here: press the arrow keys, or tap the pad.

Specials

On a keyboard, press two arrows together for the diagonals, like ↑ + → for a cover drive. Shown for a right-handed batter.

Bat on ball

Timing turns the face.

Bat on ball is a real collision. The bat has mass and swing speed, the ball comes off the face, and friction puts spin on it.

  • Sweet spotHit it off the toe or the splice and it comes off slower and lower.
  • EdgesOutside edges fly to the slips. Inside edges can go on to the stumps.
  • TimingEarly goes to the leg side, late to the off side. Late on a drive sends it up.
  • LBWDecided like ball tracking: where it pitched, whether it was in line, and whether it would hit.

Free nets

Three bowling levels: Club, Pro and International. Bat right- or left-handed.

Under the lights

A full ground, not a practice net.

A captain who moves the field

Eleven players and a captain who sets slips after a wicket, boundary riders when you need a lot, and fills a gap once you find it twice.

27 batting shots

Players modelled and animated in Blender: drives, cuts, pulls, hooks, sweeps, the reverse sweep and the scoop over the keeper.

Commentary in English or Hindi

A two-voice box reacts to fours, sixes, wickets, drops and good leaves. The umpire calls wides and no balls.

A crowd that reacts

It hushes at delivery, oohs at a play-and-miss and does a Mexican wave after a six, with dhol drums and horns.

Bat with your camera

Swipe your hand, or a brightly coloured object, in front of the camera. A rising swipe goes in the air. A faster one hits harder.

Wides, no balls and free hits

Leave the wide one, punish the no ball, and swing freely on the free hit.
