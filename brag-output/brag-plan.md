# Air Cricket — brag plan

**What it is:** a 3D floodlit cricket batting game that runs in a browser tab (and as an Android app).
**For:** anyone who wants to face a few overs on their laptop or phone, with keys, touch, or their camera.
**What sets it apart:** real bat-on-ball physics (edges fly to slips, timing turns the face), Blender-rigged players with 27 shots, a captain who moves the field, a living crowd, and two-voice commentary in English or Hindi. All in one HTML page.
**Most impressive claim:** a daily challenge where everyone in the world faces the same balls.
**Visual hook:** the real game: a lofted six under the floodlights, with the game's own slow-mo hit-stop.
**Share caption:** see `share-copy.txt`.

**Tone:** `default` (punchy, playful, clean), coloured like the game's stadium LED board.
**Identity:** night `#08161b`, LED amber `#ffb627`, chalk `#eef1e7`, four `#83e3a6`, six `#8fd6ff`; Big Shoulders Display + Barlow Semi Condensed.
**Footage:** captured frame by frame from the real `index.html` in headless Chromium under a virtual clock, with shots played through the game's own `requestShot()`.
