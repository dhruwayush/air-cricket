Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated 7 October 2026

Air Cricket has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. Your scores and settings stay on your device, and camera video is processed on your device and never sent anywhere.

This policy covers the Air Cricket game in the browser (dhruwayush.github.io/air-cricket), the Air Cricket Android app, and this website (air-cricket.vercel.app). "We" means the developer of Air Cricket.

What we collect

Nothing. The game doesn't ask for your name, email or any other personal details, and it doesn't send your scores, settings or gameplay to us or anyone else. There is no sign-in and no tracking.

What stays on your device

The game saves a few things in your browser's local storage (or the app's storage on Android) so they're there next time you play:

ItemWhat it holds
ac-recordsYour daily challenge results, winning streak and wins in each mode
ac-soundWhether sound is on or off
ac-voiceCommentary language: English, Hindi or off
ac-gfx, ac-gfx-autoYour graphics setting, and whether Auto switched to Fast on your device
ac-fieldmapWhether the field map is shown

None of this leaves your device. To delete it, clear the site data for the game in your browser settings, or on Android go to Settings → Apps → Air Cricket → Storage → Clear storage.

Camera

The camera is used only if you choose Camera · hand or Camera · object as bat in the menu. Your browser or Android will ask for permission first, and you can say no and play with keys or touch instead.

  • Video frames are processed on your device to follow your hand or the coloured object, and turned into shots.
  • Video is never recorded, saved or uploaded.
  • The camera turns off when you switch back to keyboard or touch, or close the game.

The Android app asks for the CAMERA permission for this, and INTERNET to download the hand-tracking model. You can turn the camera permission off in Android settings at any time.

Outside services the game loads from

Like most websites, the game loads some files from other servers. Those servers see the request, which includes your IP address and browser details, as part of serving the file. We don't control how they handle those logs.

The Android app has the 3D library and fonts built in, so it only goes online to download the hand-tracking model.

Sharing your result

"Share result" copies your score line to your clipboard, or opens your phone's share sheet. Nothing is shared unless you choose where to send it.

Children

Air Cricket is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal information, it collects none from children either.

Changes

If the game starts handling data differently, we'll update this page and the date at the top before the change goes live.
